04 / Behind the decks

Two DJs.
A thousand destinations.

Aerolíneas Zeta brings together two DJs, Charlie and Miquel, and one shared passion: making songs from different worlds speak the same language.

Curiosity drives us, and we love mixing it all, whatever the style. Digging through the underground, uncovering a hidden gem and pairing it with that mainstream anthem we know by heart. From indie to electronic, pop to rock, following the thread that connects one song to the next.

Every set is a journey through discoveries and memories. Bootlegs and mashups are our way of charting new routes. The destination is always the dance floor.
